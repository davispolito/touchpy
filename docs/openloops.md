**Standing rules:**

1. **Done items exit immediately.** Move completed, superseded, or mooted work — with
   verification evidence — to [changelog](changelog.md) in the same edit.
2. **Items stay short.** Use a bold title, dated status/verification stamp, one next
   physical action, and a link to detail. Keep long evidence elsewhere.
